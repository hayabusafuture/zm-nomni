Spending (based on invoice data)

Spending per outletSee how much your outlets are spending over a period of time or on specific days. Spending per supplierUse this to match with your Statement of Accounts or see if you have exceeded spending budget for the supplier. Spending per SKUSee what SKUs you are spending most on, or how frequently you order it. You can also view the price history of the items. Spending per categoryFind out the type of items you are purchasing. Spending per tagSee the breakdown of your spending based on how you've tagged the SKUs. Spending per expense categoryView your spending based on the expense categories you've created.

Variance

3-way matchCompares invoice with both Goods Received Note (GRN) and the order to highlight any variances. Price changesShows all changes in price recorded in the invoices. 2-way match (Invoice variance)Compares invoice value with the original order to highlight any variances. Receiving reportCompares data of received items (GRN) with the purchase orders.

Inventory

Cost of Goods SoldReview the cost of goods sold across your outlets and reporting period. Inventory on handSee the value and quantity of stock recorded in your inventory. Theoretical Cost of Goods SoldCompare expected food cost with actual performance for the period. Inventory movementReview stock received, used, transferred and adjusted over time.
