Approval workflow:
  • Orders created by users with lower level will need approval from users at higher levels regardless of the order value.
  • 'Basic' users are always in lowest user level.
  • Users without the 'Approve orders' permission are skipped in the approval workflow, regardless of their user level or order limit.
  • Orders over the value of user's limit has to be approved by a user with higher limit (if there are any).
  • If the outlet does not require approval workflow, leave 'order limit' for all users empty.
  • 'Basic' users always require approval and have order limit of $0.
  • Users without the 'Approve orders' permission are skipped in the approval workflow, regardless of their user level or order limit.
Orders to the selected suppliers do not require any approval. Basic users will not be able to place orders to these suppliers.
